# 4. Staff go-lives by skill, language and load; the lead steps in on projects heading late

**Status:** accepted

Many delivery teams give a new customer to whoever has the fewest projects. The desk scores each manager on the
integration skills the customer needs (DMS family, identity provider), the customer's language, and weeks of open work,
and avoids loading anyone past a limit. Each week the lead finds projects whose projected finish passes the date promised
at kickoff and spends their own hours there first, earliest promise first, and escalates once to the customer's sponsor.

The ablations in the results show what each part is worth on the same customers; skill matching alone is the smallest
of the four.
